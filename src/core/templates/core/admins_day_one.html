Admin Guide

Everything here starts from Members, at the top of every page.

1. Invite A Household

A household is one home. One link covers everyone in it.

  1. Members → Invite A Household.
  2. Name the household and choose which side it joins.
  3. Send the link however you normally reach them.

The link is shown once. Make another from Invites.

{% comment %} R2-6, one sentence. Most admins reading this will never mint a side-admin link — only the family admin is offered the control — but every admin reading this may have ARRIVED through one, and a link that hands out authority should not be a thing the product never mentions. {% endcomment %}

Only the Family Admin can hand a side over. The first person to join with that link becomes the Side Admin, and everyone after them joins as a member.

2. Add A Grandparent

Post something to their side first, or they will open the link to an empty page. You cannot preview it for them.

  1. For someone new: Members → Add A Grandparent.
  2. For someone already here: tap Manage on their row and then No-Login Link.

Anyone holding the link can read and send love as them. Hand it over in person or in a private message, never in a group chat.

3. When Someone Is Locked Out

  1. If they have an email address, Forgot Your Password? on the sign-in page does it without you.
  2. If not, tap Manage on their row, then Sign-In Link. It works once and expires after two days.

Make sure you are really talking to them first. Call or text the number you already have.

4. Move Or Remove Someone

Both live under Manage on their row.

  • Change Household moves someone into another household, or out of one.
  • Remove asks what happens to what they wrote: keep their posts, keep them without their name, or delete them.

Deleting erases their photos from the server for good, and asks you to type their name first. Photos other people added replying to their posts go too.

5. If You Are Locked Out

  1. With an email address on file, use Forgot Your Password?
  2. Without one, ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}whoever set this Backyard up{% endif %} for a Sign-In Link.
  3. If no admin can sign in, the password has to be reset at the server by whoever runs this Backyard.

Adding a second sign-in step under Settings is worth the minute it takes.
