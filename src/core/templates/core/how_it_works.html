How this works

A private place for our family. No ads, no strangers, nothing that nags. Post when you feel like it.

Who sees what I post

Everything you write goes to your household — the people who share your home, or whoever was set up alongside you. Nobody else sees it.

When you want more people to see something, you choose a wider group as you write it, and Backyard asks you to confirm before it goes. A side of the family is one branch of it, with its own feed: two sides never see each other.

Who can join, and how

Only by invitation. Someone who looks after this Backyard makes a link and sends it to you; you open it, pick a name and a password, and you are in. There is no way to sign up from the outside, and nothing here is public.

Adding people is an admin’s job, so if you would like somebody brought in, ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}whoever in the family set this up{% endif %} and they will make you a link to pass on.

A grandparent who would rather not deal with passwords can be given a no-login link instead. It opens straight onto a large-text page of the family's posts with one button to send love. Anyone holding that link can use it, so it is handed over in person or by a private message.

The Family email

If you want one, Backyard can email you what the family has been up to, weekly or monthly. It is off unless you turn it on, it goes only to an address you have confirmed, and a week when nobody posted sends you nothing at all.

Every one of those emails has a link at the bottom that stops them. You can also stop them here at any time, and stopping the email never removes you from the family.

My photos

Photos live on this family's own server and are shown only to the people the post was for. They are not sent to any other company, they are not used to train anything, and they are never scanned or sold.

You can delete any post of yours: open it, choose Delete, and its photos are removed from the server for good. The one thing nobody can recall is a copy that already went out in a Family email, the same as any email you have already sent.

You can also take a copy of everything you have written and every photo you have added, whenever you like.

What this place keeps about me

One thing, and this is it: once a week, whether you stopped by. Just a yes or a no. It is there so the family can tell this place is still alive.

Backyard does not record what you read, how long you stayed, or what you tapped. There is no per-person activity list anywhere in it. The only numbers anyone can see are totals for a whole side of the family, never one of us on our own.

If I forget my password

If you gave an email address, use Forgot your password? on the sign-in page and Backyard will email you a way back in.

If you did not give one, ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}whoever in the family set this up{% endif %}. They can make you a one-time link that lets you choose a new password. It is worth adding an email address now so you never need to ask.
