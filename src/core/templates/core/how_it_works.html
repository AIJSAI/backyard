How It Works

What Backyard stores, who can see it, and how to leave.

Who Sees What You Post

Posts are shared with your household: the people you live with, or whoever was set up alongside you. Nobody else sees them.

A side of the family is one branch of it, with its own feed. A group is a set of people somebody picked.

To reach further than your household, choose a side or a group when you post. Posting to a side shows you how many people will see it and asks you to confirm.

Most people are on one side and see only that side. A household that belongs to both sides sees both, and that is the only way a post reaches the other side.

Who Can Join

By invitation only. An admin creates an invite link and sends it to you. Open it to choose your name, a username and a password. There is no public sign-up, and no post is public.

Only an admin can invite somebody. Ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}the person who invited you{% endif %} for a link to pass on.

Somebody who would rather not use a password can be given a No-Login Link instead. It opens a large-text page of recent posts with one button to send love. Anyone holding that link can read and send love as that person, so hand it over in person or in a private message.

Admins

Two roles manage people. A Side Admin adds and removes members on one side. A Family Admin manages everyone, on both sides.

Admins manage people, not what you can read. An admin sees a post only if it was shared with them, the same as everyone else.

An admin can take down a post they can see. That is permanent, and the author is not told.

Email Updates

Backyard can email you what has been posted, weekly or monthly. It is off until you turn it on.

Updates go only to an address you have confirmed. If nobody posted, nothing is sent.

Every update carries a link that stops them, and you can stop them in Settings. Stopping them does not remove you from Backyard.

Updates are delivered by an email provider, the same as any other email.

Photos

Photos are stored on this server and shown only to the people the post was shared with. Backyard does not send them to another company and does not use them to train anything.

To delete a post, find it in your feed and choose Delete Post. The post and its photos are removed from the server permanently.

Photos are never attached to an email update. Text that has already been emailed cannot be recalled.

What Backyard Stores About You

Your posts, replies, photos and profile details, the name you sign in with, and an email address if you gave one.

Backyard does not record what you read, how long you stayed, or what you tapped. There is no per-person activity list. The only numbers anyone can see are totals for a whole side, never one person.

There are no ads and no third-party tracking.

You can download everything you have posted at any time.

If You Forget Your Password

If you gave an email address and confirmed it, use Forgot Your Password? on the sign-in page.

If you did not, ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}the person who invited you{% endif %} for a Sign-In Link. Use it once to choose a new password.

Adding an email address and confirming it means you can reset your password yourself.

If You Want To Leave

You can leave a group yourself on the Groups page. A household is managed by an admin.

To leave Backyard, ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}the person who invited you{% endif %} to remove you. They choose whether your posts stay as they are, stay without your name, or are deleted along with their photos. Download your data first if you want a copy.
