How It Works

Backyard keeps everyone connected and up to date on a private network: birthdays, trips, big events. It works like a private Instagram.

Posting

Share words, photos or videos from the top of your feed. A post goes to your household unless you pick a group or add a side under Who Can See This.

  • Household: the people you live with.
  • Side: one side of the family. Posting to a side shows how many people will see it and asks you to confirm.
  • Group: people somebody picked, like the cousins or a trip.

You see the sides your household and your groups are on. Signed in, you can reply to any post you can see and react to it.

Photos are shown only to the people the post was shared with. You can edit a post for fifteen minutes. Delete Post removes it and its photos for good.

On Your Phone

Add Backyard to your home screen and it opens like an app. There's nothing to download from an app store.

Joining

Backyard is invitation only. An admin sends a link, and you open it to choose a name, a username and a password. To get someone invited, ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}the person who invited you{% endif %}.

{% comment %} "send love", not "react": that is what the button on that page says, and it is what both admin pages that hand out the link say. {% endcomment %}

For someone who won't use a password, an admin can make a No-Login Link. It opens a large-text page of recent posts with one button to send love. Whoever holds it can read and send love as that person, so send it privately.

Admins

Admins add and remove people: a Side Admin on their side, a Family Admin everywhere. They see only the posts shared with them, the same as everyone else. An admin can take down a post they can see. That is permanent, and the author is not told.

Email Updates

Get new posts by email, weekly or monthly. It's off until you turn it on, and it goes to an address you've confirmed. If nobody posted, nothing is sent. Every update has a link to stop them. Photos stay on Backyard: the email links to them.

Your Data

Backyard keeps your posts, replies, photos and profile, your username, and your email address if you add one. You can download your posts, replies and photos.

Leave a group on the Groups page. To leave Backyard, ask {% if help_contact %}{{ help_contact }}{% else %}the person who invited you{% endif %} to remove you. Your posts can stay, stay without your name, or be deleted along with their photos. Download your data first if you want a copy.
