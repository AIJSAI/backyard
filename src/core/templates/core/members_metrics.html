A weekly count of posts and replies for each side of the family. We record whether someone visited in a week, never what they looked at, and no number here is about any one person.

{% comment %} SAY WHY A COLUMN READS ZERO (walk item 33). "Replied by email" counts replies that arrived as e-mail, and since #101 stopped putting a per-post reply address in the Family email there is no way for a relative to send one — nothing hands anybody an address. The column is kept rather than dropped because the inbound pipeline is still armed and still tested, and a row from before the change carries a real number; but an admin reading a permanent zero deserves to know it is a retired route and not a family that stopped replying. {% endcomment %}

“Replied by email” is a retired route: replying to the Family email was turned off, so this column counts nothing new and reads zero for every week since. Replies made on the web are counted under “Posts with a reply”.
