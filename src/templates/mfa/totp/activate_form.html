Setting up an authenticator app. The stock page opened with "To protect your account with two-factor authentication, scan the QR code below with your authenticator app. Then, input the verification code generated by the app below." — one sentence carrying three instructions, "input" as a verb, and "below" twice. The key is still offered, because somebody setting this up on the same phone cannot scan a square on that phone's own screen and has to type it in. Its sentence says what it is FOR rather than what it is ("You can store this secret and use it to reinstall your authenticator app at a later time."). The read-only key box, the `code` field name and the form action are the package's. The picture is NOT: allauth draws it as a `data:` whose ALT text is the key itself, character for character repeating the box underneath. It is inline SVG here, described once by the wrapper's aria-label.
