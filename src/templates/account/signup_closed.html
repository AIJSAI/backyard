Signup is invite-only here (S-101) and the adapter refuses the route, so allauth renders this page instead — headed "Sign Up Closed" and apologising in the first person plural: "We are sorry, but the sign up is currently closed." There is no "we", nothing is closed, and nothing has gone wrong: this product simply has one way in. Nobody is linked here. It is reached by typing the address, or by an old link, or by a search result. It deliberately does NOT repeat the entrance layout's lede, which sits three lines above it and already says "Backyard is invite-only. Open your invite link to join." Two lines saying one thing is the defect this pass was called for.
