{% comment %} SIGNING OUT TAKES THE NOTIFICATIONS WITH IT (S-107), on this device only. A phone gets handed round a family. Without this, signing out on a borrowed phone leaves a live push registration behind: the next person holding it reads " posted" and the first words of a post on the lock screen, for a Backyard they cannot open. That is the same class of leak the service worker caches nothing to avoid (ADR-002), arriving by a different road. BEST EFFORT, and never a reason the sign-out does not happen. The form is submitted from here whatever the unsubscribe does, including when there is no registration, no PushManager, or the request fails -- a member who cannot reach the network must still be able to sign out. The server row goes first (so it stops being sent to even if the browser's own teardown fails) and the registration second. Only THIS device's row: the endpoint identifies one browser profile, so a relative's other phone keeps its notifications. {% endcomment %} {% endblock content %}
