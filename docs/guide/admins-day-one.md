# Admin Guide

The same text as the in-product page at `/members/day-one/`, reachable from **Members**. It
lives here too so it can be read and reviewed without a running server; the page is the copy
the family actually sees, and the two are kept together by `test_admins_day_one.py`. The
page names whoever runs this Backyard in sections 5 and 6, read from the database at render
time; a file in a repository cannot, so this copy says it in general terms.

---

Admins add people, help them sign in, and remove them. It all starts from **Members**, at the
top of every page.

## 1. Invite A Household

A household is one home. One link covers everyone in it.

1. **Members → Invite A Household**.
2. Name the household and choose which side it joins.
3. Text or email them the link.

The link is shown once. Make another from **Invites**.

Only the Family Admin can hand a side over. The first person to join with that link becomes
an Admin, and everyone after them joins as a member.

## 2. Add A Grandparent

**Post something to their side first**, or the link opens to an empty page.

1. For someone new: **Members → Add A Grandparent**.
2. For someone already here: tap **Manage** on their row and then **No-Login Link**.

Anyone holding the link can read and send love as them. Hand it over in person or in a
private message, never in a group chat.

## 3. When Someone Is Locked Out

1. Ask them to try **Forgot Your Password?** on the sign-in page. It works only for an email
   address they have confirmed.
2. If no email arrives, tap **Manage** on their row, then **Sign-In Link**. It works once and
   expires after two days.

**Make sure you are really talking to them first.** Call or text the number you already
have.

## 4. Move Or Remove Someone

Both live under **Manage** on their row.

- **Change Household** moves someone into another household, or out of one.
- **Remove** asks what happens to what they wrote: keep their posts, keep them without
  their name, or delete them.

Deleting erases their photos from the server for good, and asks you to type their name
first. Photos other people added replying to their posts go too.

## 5. If You Are Locked Out

1. With a confirmed email address, use **Forgot Your Password?**
2. Without one, ask whoever set this Backyard up for a **Sign-In Link**.
3. If no admin can sign in, the password has to be reset at the server by whoever runs this
   Backyard.

Add a second sign-in step under Settings, in Passkeys And Sign-In Codes.

## 6. If You Are Stuck

Read **How It Works**. If it does not answer the question, ask whoever set this
Backyard up.
