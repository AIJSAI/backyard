# Honest 100% audit — 2026-07-26

Seven read-only lenses over the shipped product versus the founding documents (PR-FAQ,
principles, founding research, story map, threat model, assumptions), each finding then
adversarially verified. Run after the founder observed that ordinary questions kept surfacing
gaps no checklist contained.

**Every lens returned `not-ready`.** This document is the raw finding set; the prioritised plan
lives in docs/PATH-TO-100.md.

## Headline

Totals: **15 blocker, 32 high, 32 medium, 11 low.**

The product does not deliver its central promise. **A grandparent on the no-login path cannot see
a single photograph anywhere** — not on the elder page, not in the digest email, not on the digest
web view. The PR-FAQ's entire elder argument rests on a study about showing grandma photos.
Compounding it: `serve_media` is `@login_required` and token-only elders have `user_id = NULL`, so
this is not a template fix — it needs a credential-free media path, which is a TM-9 security-surface
change.

Several stories marked `passing` are provably not: **S-501** (its acceptance names "inline images"),
**S-702** (delete a member's content), **S-802** (encrypted backups), **S-901** (profile fields),
**S-103** (PWA identity/install).

## Findings by lens

### LENS 1 — Does the shipped product deliver what docs/PR-FAQ.md promises?

**Verdict: not-ready**

_Genuinely done:_ A large share of the PR-FAQ is genuinely delivered and I verified it in the running app, not in code. Pods and yards scope every post and separateness holds (compose_confirm.html forces an explicit named confirmation for any audience wider than your own pod, TM-3). The family directory with kinship names and calmly-surfaced birthdays is live. Member self-export produces a real 46KB zip on demand at /settings/export/. AGPL-3.0 is in the tree. And the design pass is real: prod serves the v3 system on every surface I fetched.

Post 2 'This is the bread recipe I have been going on about' has a link_preview row (https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/no-knead-crusty-white-bread-recipe) but its body contains no URL, so on her screen the recipe is unreachable text. None of the 3 replies to the fence post appear on her page (grepped each comment body against the rendered HTML: 0 matches). Same omission in docs/../digest_web.html:6-14 and digest_post.html:5-8. Against docs/PR-FAQ.md:15 ('a 21-week in-home trial of a one-way "just show grandma the photos" display failed until its redesign let grandma post back... #### [BLOCKER · m] There is no photo viewer anywhere — tapping a photo leaves the app and dumps the raw image file

- **Evidence:** src/core/templates/core/feed.html:76 and src/core/templates/core/post_detail.html:25 are identical: `<a href="{% url 'serve_media' m.token %}"><img src="{% url 'serve_media' m.thumbnail_token %}" ...></a>`. No lightbox, no next/previous, no caption, no close, no download affordance, no way back except the browser's back button (and the app renders no in-page back link on that dead-end).

Priya's five-photo camp album has no way to be flipped through: five separate taps, five hard exits from the app, five browser-back presses. On an iPhone in standalone PWA mode this is worse — navigating to an image/jpeg inside the installed app leaves the family with a full-screen image and no chrome at all.
- **Fix:** Build a photo viewer: a full-screen overlay (or a server-rendered /posts/<id>/photos/<n>/ page if you want it JS-free and elder-safe) with next/previous, the alt text or post body as the caption, a large close control, and swipe on touch. Keep serve_media as the byte path so the audience re-check is untouched.
